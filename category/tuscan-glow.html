Tuscan Glow

Tuscan Glow is inspired by the golden light that washes over rolling hills and stone villas at sunset. This collection captures the warmth of Tuscany — sunlit terraces, ripening citrus groves, and the soft hum of late summer evenings.

Bright notes of lemon zest and bergamot open into green olive leaf and sweet fig, resting on a gentle base of cypress wood and warm amber. The fragrance feels radiant yet grounded, fresh yet softly enveloping.

Tuscan Glow brings a luminous, relaxed elegance into your space — like a quiet evening in the Italian countryside, where everything is bathed in honeyed light. 🕯🍋
