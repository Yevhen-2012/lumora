Returns and Exchanges

Introduction

At Lumora, we strive to ensure that you are completely satisfied with your purchase. Our candles are carefully handcrafted using high-quality wax and thoughtfully blended fragrances to create a warm and atmospheric experience. However, we understand that occasionally a product may not meet your expectations. This policy outlines our returns and exchanges process.

Returns

  1. Eligibility for Return: Items must be returned within 30 days of the purchase date. To be eligible for a return, the candle must be unused, unburned, and in the same condition that you received it, including its original packaging.
  2. Return Process: To initiate a return, please contact us at Lumora customer support with your order number and details about the product you wish to return. Our team will provide instructions on how to proceed.
  3. Refunds: Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you via email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, the refund will be processed and automatically applied to your original method of payment within several business days.

Exchanges

  1. Eligibility for Exchange: We replace items only if they are defective, damaged during shipping, or if the wrong product was sent. If you need to exchange an item for the same product, please contact Lumora customer support.
  2. Exchange Process: Please contact us first to confirm that your item is eligible for exchange. Items sent back without prior communication may not be accepted.

Shipping Returns/Exchanges

  1. Shipping Costs: Customers are responsible for paying their own shipping costs for returning items unless the product arrived damaged or defective. Shipping costs are non-refundable. If a refund is issued, the cost of return shipping may be deducted from the refund.
  2. Shipping Address: Please send your approved return or exchange to the address provided by Lumora customer support after your request has been reviewed.

Non-Returnable Items

  1. Exceptions: Some items may not be eligible for return, including:
    • Used or burned candles
    • Custom or personalized candles
    • Gift cards
    • Items marked as final sale
    Due to the handcrafted nature of Lumora candles, slight variations in color, fragrance intensity, or finish may occur and are not considered defects.

Additional Information

  1. Questions: If you have any questions regarding our Returns and Exchanges Policy, please contact Lumora customer support through the contact page on our website.
