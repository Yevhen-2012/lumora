Lemon & Olive Leaf

Lemon & Olive Leaf opens with bright citrus zest and crisp green olive leaf, capturing the freshness of sunlit Mediterranean groves. Subtle herbal undertones and soft woods add balance, creating a scent that feels clean, airy, and naturally refined. Fresh yet grounding, this fragrance brings light, warmth, and effortless coastal elegance into your space.

• Hand-poured natural soy wax for a clean, even burn.

• Premium cotton wick for a steady, smoke-free flame.

• Size S — 120 g | Burn time: approximately 25–30 hours.

• Size M — 220 g | Burn time: approximately 45–50 hours.

• Size L — 350 g | Burn time: approximately 70–80 hours.

• Fragrance profile: fresh lemon zest, green olive leaf, soft herbs, light wood undertones.

• Reusable matte ceramic vessel.

• Shipping: Ships worldwide. Delivery times vary based on location.
